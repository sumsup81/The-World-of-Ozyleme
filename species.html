Species of Ozyleme

Many peoples walk beneath the hollow sun of Ozyleme. Some were born here, some were created, and some became something other than what they once were.

Though many may resemble peoples known in other worlds, their origins, cultures, and place within Ozyleme can be dramatically different.

The Reborn

When a creature dies, its Essence of Experience does not always fully depart.

Sometimes those lingering echoes return to the body. What awakens may resemble the person who died, but the being that returns is not truly the same person.

The Others

Not every creature found beneath the hollow sun fits neatly into the commonly recognized peoples of Ozyleme.

Rare bloodlines, travelers from impossible places, unusual transformations, forgotten creations, and other exceptional beings may be found here.
