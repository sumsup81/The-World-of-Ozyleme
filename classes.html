Classes of Ozyleme

Adventurers beneath the hollow sun follow many familiar paths, though magic, the gods, the Dragon King, and the strange laws of Ozyleme may alter what those paths mean.

Notes concerning each class and its place within Ozyleme will be recorded here as the world grows.

Choose a Class

Artificer

Inventors, craftsmen, experimenters, and wielders of strange creations.

Barbarian

Warriors who draw terrible strength from fury, instinct, and primal power.

Bard

Performers, storytellers, wanderers, and wielders of magic shaped through expression.

Cleric

Mortal servants who draw power from divine forces and the gods of Ozyleme.

Druid

Keepers of ancient natural powers found within Ozyleme's forests, wastes, fields, and wild places.

Fighter

Soldiers, warriors, guards, duelists, mercenaries, and masters of martial combat.

Kindred

A strange path unique to those whose existence has been transformed by the powers of Ozyleme.

Monk

Disciplined warriors who turn body, mind, and supernatural focus into deadly weapons.

Paladin

Warriors empowered by powerful vows, convictions, and sacred obligations.

Ranger

Hunters, scouts, wanderers, and survivors of Ozyleme's dangerous wilderness.

Rogue

Thieves, assassins, explorers, tricksters, criminals, and masters of cunning.

Sorcerer

Those whose magic emerges from within their blood, body, spirit, or altered nature.

Warlock

Those who gain supernatural power through bargains, patrons, promises, and dangerous pacts.

Wizard

Scholars who seek to understand and manipulate the dangerous magic woven through Ozyleme.
