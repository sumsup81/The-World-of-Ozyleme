Player Guide to Ozyleme

This guide contains the knowledge, customs, beliefs, and rules needed to create a character who belongs beneath the hollow sun of Ozyleme.

You do not need to know every kingdom, god, creature, or piece of history before playing. Begin with what your character would reasonably know and allow the darker mysteries of Ozyleme to reveal themselves during play.

Create a Character

Use the resources below when building a character for Ozyleme. Standard Dungeons & Dragons character creation applies unless an Ozyleme rule specifically changes it.

All characters in Ozyleme gain +1 to one ability score of their choice.

How to Roleplay in Ozyleme

Characters in Ozyleme have grown up in a world where strange skies, cruel gods, dangerous wilderness, unusual peoples, and supernatural forces are parts of ordinary life.

Remember What Is Normal Here

Things that appear grotesque or supernatural to an outsider may be completely ordinary to someone born in Ozyleme.

The Dragon King

The Dragon King occupies a place in Ozyleme unlike that of any mortal ruler. His presence reaches beyond kingdoms, borders, generations, and even the gods themselves.

Not everything said about the Dragon King is necessarily true. Some things are taught as history. Others survive as religion, warning, superstition, rumor, or forbidden story.
