Welcome to Ozyleme

Beneath a sickly sky and the light of a hollow white sun lies Ozyleme, a world divided among eight kingdoms and watched by ancient gods.

Beyond their borders lie black deserts, mist-shrouded wilderness, strange peoples, forgotten ruins, and the distant shadow of the Dragon King.

The Essence of Experience

In the world of Ozyleme, all creatures carry the Essence of Experience. It is the source of their animation, their motivations, their struggles, and their growth.

When a creature is dispatched, some measure of that essence is released into the world for others to claim. Those who train in the skilled classes of occupation learn to turn that essence into something greater.

A sorcerer may draw it deep into the bones until new power ignites within them. A barbarian may feed it into rage until flesh and fury become terrible strength. However it is used, Experience is more than a tally of deeds. It is power carried by the living.

Your Friend in a Hat knows this very well.

He is quite willing to take certain treasures from your hands, particularly magical ones, and return something considerably more useful: Experience.

Trading Treasure for Experience

The greater the treasure surrendered, the less efficiently its value can be drawn out as Experience.

Treasure Value Exchange Experience
First 100 gp Full 1 XP per 1 gp
101 to 500 gp 1/2 1 XP per 2 gp
501 to 5,000 gp 1/4 1 XP per 4 gp
5,001 to 50,000 gp 1/8 1 XP per 8 gp
More than 50,000 gp 1/16 1 XP per 16 gp
The exchange is progressive. Each portion of a treasure's value is exchanged at the rate belonging to that portion. A costly relic therefore does not suddenly lose the value of its first few hundred gold pieces.
Gemstones and other treasures with an established gold-piece value use the same exchange. Ordinary coinage is not enough. The Friend in a Hat is interested in treasures worthy of the exchange.

Spending Experience

Experience belongs to the character who earns it. How that power is used is their choice.

Experience may be used to advance in a class, pursue another class, strengthen equipment, awaken new properties in an item, purchase a Soul Deed, or take part in other bargains where Experience is accepted.

A character is not required to spend Experience immediately. It may be carried, saved, or spent as the player chooses.

Multiclassing

A character may use their Experience to begin another class, but such knowledge is not taken freely in Ozyleme. Before gaining a level in a new class, the character must seek permission from the power capable of granting or recognizing that path.

This permission may come from the appropriate God of Ozyleme, or from Ozyleme himself. It may be sought through ritual, prayer, sacrifice, pilgrimage, service, or another act suited to the power being petitioned.

What is demanded, and whether permission is granted, is determined through play. Experience provides the power to walk the new path. The gods decide whether the path is opened.

Experience may also be surrendered to strengthen equipment, awaken new properties, or bind elemental powers into an item. Such alterations may include greater magical bonuses, additional elemental damage, or resistance to an element.

Experience is yours to spend. Characters may save or use their earned XP as they wish, provided they meet any requirements attached to what they seek to gain.
A character can never lose a level by spending XP. Only Experience earned beyond the minimum required for the character's current level may be spent.

Soul Deeds

Some bargains are meant for possessions. Others are meant for the possessor.

Your Friend in a Hat can provide a Soul Deed, a peculiar contract protecting its bearer when death reaches farther than ordinary death saves can follow.

Character Level Soul Deed Cost
Levels 1 to 4 500 XP
Levels 5 to 8 1,500 XP
Levels 9 to 12 3,000 XP
Levels 13 to 16 5,000 XP
Levels 17 to 20 7,500 XP
An unused Soul Deed may be traded in when its bearer outgrows it. The value already paid is credited toward the stronger deed, and only the difference in XP must be surrendered.
