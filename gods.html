The Eight Gods

Eight gods hold power across the kingdoms of Ozyleme. Each is bound to a force of the world and holds mastery over a particular domain.

They are worshipped, feared, bargained with, celebrated, and cursed by the people who live beneath the hollow sun. Yet even the gods exist beneath the authority of the Dragon King.
