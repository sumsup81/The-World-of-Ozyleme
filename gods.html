The Eight Gods

Eight gods watch over the kingdoms of Ozyleme. Each holds dominion over a different part of the world and the lives of those who dwell beneath the hollow sun.

They are powerful, feared, worshipped, and bargained with, yet even the gods exist beneath the shadow and authority of the Dragon King.
